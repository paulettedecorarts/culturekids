Cultural Background

The Buganda Kingdom is the largest traditional kingdom in Uganda, located in the Central region. The Baganda people are known for their clan system (amasiga), rich oral traditions, and the Lubiri (royal palace) at Kasubi.

Key Proverb

"Omuntu wa bantu"

A person is a person through other people. Community above all.
